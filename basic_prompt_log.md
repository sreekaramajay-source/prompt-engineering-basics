## Task 1: Text Summarization

### Prompt Input

```text
Summarize the text below in exactly three bullet points.

Text: "Remote work has rapidly expanded, introducing both opportunities and obstacles for modern businesses..."
```

### ChatGPT Output

- Remote work has expanded rapidly and changed how many businesses operate.
- It can create opportunities such as flexibility and access to a wider talent pool.
- Businesses may also face challenges coordinating teams, communicating effectively, and maintaining collaboration.

## Task 4: Tone Editing

### Prompt Input

```text
Act as an Editor. Rewrite the draft text below to sound like a professional, polite B2B email sent to a client.

Draft: "hey, sorry we are late on the report. it had bugs. we will send it tomorrow. thanks."
```

### ChatGPT Output

**Subject: Update on Report Delivery**

Dear [Client Name],

I apologize for the delay in delivering the report. We identified a few issues during our review and are working to resolve them. We expect to send you the completed report tomorrow.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]

## Task 5: Constraint-Driven Output

### Prompt Input

```text
Summarize the sentence below in under 20 words. Do not use the word "help" or "assist".

Sentence: "Our customer success managers are available 24 hours a day to guide new users and resolve technical account disputes."
```

### ChatGPT Output

Customer success managers are available 24/7 to guide users and resolve technical account disputes.
